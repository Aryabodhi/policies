Gravity Well TD - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

Gravity Well TD ("the game") is a puzzle tower-defence game published by Cabal Of Clues ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and who handles it. Contact: asinc.andi.app@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no online profile. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location, and we do not run our own servers that store information about you.

Your progress (stars per level, unlocked levels, the reduced-effects setting and whether you bought Remove Ads) is stored only on your device. We do not receive it. If you uninstall the game, this data is removed from your device.

2. Advertising (Unity LevelPlay)

The game shows full-screen ads between levels and optional rewarded video ads (for example, watching an ad to retry a level with an extra gravity well). Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service of Unity Technologies, which may request ads from Unity Ads and from other advertising networks it works with. To deliver, cap and measure ads, LevelPlay and these networks may collect and process, for example, your device's advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device and operating system information, and information about how you interact with ads. This is handled under their own privacy policies; see Unity's at unity.com/legal/privacy-policy, which also lists its LevelPlay partners.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). Buying "Remove Ads" removes the full-screen ads between levels; optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch are not part of that removal.

3. In-app purchase (Google Play)

The game offers one optional one-time purchase, "Remove Ads". Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details; we only receive confirmation from Google Play that the purchase was made, so the game can turn off ads. See Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers described above (Unity LevelPlay and its ad network partners for advertising, Google Play for purchases) as needed to run the game.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help.

6. Retention and deletion

We do not hold personal data about you. Data stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game. For data held by Unity, its ad partners or Google, please use the controls and contact details in their policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California) you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data and to object to certain processing. Because we do not hold your data, please contact the providers named above for requests about advertising data, or write to us and we will point you to the right place.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do. The current version is always at this address.
